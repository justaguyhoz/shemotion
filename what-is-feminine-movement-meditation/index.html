FEMININE MOVEMENT MEDITATION

What Is Feminine Movement Meditation?

Feminine movement meditation is a guided movement practice that uses simple movement, intuitive expression and grounding meditation to help women reconnect with their bodies and become more present.

It is not a dance class. There is no choreography to learn and no right way to move.

Shemotion sessions are women-only, and no dance experience is required.

Movement as Meditation

Traditional meditation often asks you to become still. Feminine movement meditation begins with movement instead.

Rather than focusing on how movement looks, the attention is placed on how it feels. Guided prompts help you notice your breath, body, tension, energy and emotions while allowing movement to develop naturally.

For women who find it difficult to switch off, sit still or get out of their heads, movement can provide another way to become present.

Three connected stages

How a Shemotion Session Works

Katty guides each stage while you choose the pace and range of movement that feel right for you.

01

Guided movement meditation

Begin with simple flowing movements and coaching prompts that help warm the body, release physical tension and bring attention back to the present moment.

02

Freestyle movement meditation

Move into more intuitive expression at your own pace. Movement may be soft, playful, powerful or still. There is no choreography, judgement or pressure to perform.

03

Grounding meditation

Finish with calming movement and guided meditation to settle the body and create space to pause before returning to the rest of your day.

Is It a Dance Class?

Shemotion does not teach routines, steps or dance technique. You are not expected to follow choreography or perform for anyone.

Movement is used as a form of meditation and personal expression. Some guidance is provided, but each woman is encouraged to respond in a way that feels comfortable for her own body.

You do not need dance experience, flexibility or a particular fitness level.

Space to move your way

Who Is Feminine Movement Meditation For?

Shemotion is designed for women who want a supportive space to move, slow down and reconnect with themselves.

  • Women who spend a lot of time in their heads
  • Women who feel physically tense or mentally busy
  • Women who want to move without worrying about technique
  • Women curious about meditation but who find stillness difficult
  • Women who want time for themselves in a supportive group environment

No previous movement or meditation experience is required.

What to Expect at Your First Shemotion Session

Sessions are women-only, and Katty guides the group from beginning to end. You can come alone or with a friend and simply wear clothing that is comfortable enough to move in.

You are always welcome to modify a movement, make it smaller, pause or stop. There is no pressure to share personal experiences with the group.

Session details and what to bring may vary by event, so check the information provided for the session you choose.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need dance experience?

No. Shemotion is not a dance class and no choreography is taught.

Do I need meditation experience?

No. Katty guides the session from beginning to end.

What if I feel self-conscious moving?

There is no expectation to move in a particular way. You can keep your movements small, pause or rest whenever you need to.

Can I come alone?

Yes. Many women attend Shemotion events on their own.

Is Shemotion women-only?

Yes. Shemotion sessions and events are designed as women-only experiences.
