Shemotion for Organisations

A different kind of women's wellbeing experience, shared.

Bring women-only guided movement to your workplace, event or gathering. Sessions create space to step away from mental pressure, release tension and reconnect through movement.

An experiential complement to talks at wellness events, conferences, conventions, leadership gatherings, networking events and community events.

Retreats and Private Groups

Guided movement for women's retreats, private groups, celebrations, community gatherings and suitable destination experiences.

Studios and Wellbeing Spaces

Studios, gyms, yoga, Pilates and wellbeing venues can include Shemotion within their existing program of experiences for women.
