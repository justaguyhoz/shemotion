Shemotion for Organisations

A different kind of women's wellbeing experience for workplaces, women's events, conferences, retreats and private groups.

Guided feminine movement, intuitive expression and grounding meditation help women step away from routine, move freely and reconnect with themselves. There is no choreography and no dance experience is required.

Ways to bring Shemotion in

Women's Wellbeing Experiences for Groups and Events

Each session keeps the Shemotion method at its centre: guided feminine movement, intuitive movement and grounding meditation, with women free to move at their own pace.

Workplace Wellbeing

Shemotion can be delivered as part of employee wellbeing initiatives, women's wellbeing programs, internal women's networks, staff wellbeing days, International Women's Day events and team wellbeing experiences.

Women's Events and Conferences

Offer an experiential alternative or complement to traditional talks at women's wellness events, conferences, conventions, leadership gatherings, networking events and community events.

Retreats and Private Groups

Shape a guided movement experience for women's retreats, private groups, celebrations, community groups and suitable destination experiences.

Studios and Wellbeing Spaces

Studios, gyms, yoga, Pilates and wellbeing venues can host Shemotion as one use case within a broader program of experiences for women.

Location

Gold Coast and Brisbane

Shemotion is based on the Gold Coast and can support suitable workplace wellbeing programs, women's events, conferences, retreats and private group opportunities across the Gold Coast and Brisbane.
