What Is Shemotion?

Guided feminine movement, with space to make it your own.

Shemotion is a women-only movement and embodiment experience. It combines guided feminine movement, free expression, music, positive coaching prompts and grounding meditation.

Movement is a way to notice what you are carrying, release tension and shift towards how you want to feel. No choreography. No pressure to perform.

The practiceYour first sessionQuestions
01

Come Back Into Your Body

Gentle grounding movement, light stretching and breath help your attention move away from mental noise and towards physical sensation. You do not need to force stillness. The opening gives you space to notice how you feel and begin releasing what you are carrying.

02

Guidance Gives the Movement Intention

Katty's positive coaching prompts support the experience throughout. Feminine embodiment prompts help you notice what you are holding, what you want to release and how you would like to feel.

That intention gives the movement emotional direction without prescribing what it should look like: guided enough to feel supported, free enough to be yours.

03

Let the Energy Build

As the music builds, movement becomes freer and more expressive. Ecstatic movement is an influence, but Shemotion remains gently guided. You may move softly, wildly, playfully or powerfully, however feels natural to your body.

The focus is release, freedom and reconnecting with joy, without judgement or pressure to perform.

04

Return Gently to Grounding

The experience does not finish at its energetic peak. Breath, grounding, guided meditation, music and storytelling gradually bring the energy down. This closing gives you space to recognise the shift and leave feeling lighter and more grounded.

Is It a Dance Class?

No.

Shemotion does not teach routines, steps or dance technique. Dance and ecstatic movement influence the practice, but movement is used for embodiment, release, expression and emotional state change.

No dance experience is required.

Who is it for?

Women carrying stress, tension or mental noise; women missing a sense of play, expression or connection; and women curious about embodiment or meditation. A supportive women-only environment, with no previous movement or meditation experience required.

What to Expect at Your First Shemotion Session

Sessions are women-only, and Katty guides the group from beginning to end. Come alone or with a friend, wearing clothing comfortable enough to move in.

You are welcome to modify a movement, make it smaller, pause or stop. There is no pressure to share personal experiences with the group.

Session details and what to bring may vary by event, so check the information provided for the session you choose.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need dance experience?

No. Shemotion is not a dance class and no choreography is taught.

Do I need meditation experience?

No. Katty guides the session from beginning to end.

What if I feel self-conscious moving?

There is no expectation to move in a particular way. You can keep your movements small, pause or rest whenever you need to.

Can I come alone?

Yes. Many women attend Shemotion events on their own.

Is Shemotion women-only?

Yes. Shemotion sessions and events are designed as women-only experiences.
