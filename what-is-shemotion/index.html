WOMEN-ONLY MOVEMENT & EMBODIMENT

What Is Shemotion?

Shemotion is a women-only movement and embodiment experience designed to help you get out of your head, reconnect with your body and shift into a lighter, freer and more joyful state.

It combines guided feminine movement, free expressive movement, music, positive coaching prompts and grounding meditation.

There is no choreography, no pressure to perform and no right way to move.

Move. Release. Reconnect with Joy.

From thinking to feeling

Come Back Into Your Body

Women often arrive carrying mental noise, pressure, physical tension or emotional heaviness.

Shemotion begins with accessible grounding movement, light stretching and breath. Positive coaching prompts help your attention move away from constant thinking and back towards your body.

You do not need to force stillness or perform. The opening gives you space to notice how you feel, begin releasing what you are carrying and gently shift your state.

Movement with meaning

Guidance Gives the Movement Intention

Katty does not simply put music on and ask women to dance.

Positive coaching prompts

Feminine embodiment prompts support the experience throughout, helping you notice what you are holding and what you want to release.

A state to move towards

The prompts also invite you to choose how you want to feel, giving your movement emotional direction without prescribing how it should look.

The result is movement with purpose: guided enough to feel supported, free enough to be genuinely yours.

Free expression and joy

Let the Energy Build

As the experience builds, the music and movement become freer and more expressive.

Ecstatic movement is an influence, but Shemotion remains gently guided. You may move softly, wildly, playfully, powerfully or however feels natural in your body.

The objective is release, freedom, positive energy and reconnecting with joy - without choreography, judgement or pressure to perform.

The afterglow

Return Gently to Grounding

The experience does not finish at its energetic peak.

The energy is gradually brought back down through breath, grounding, guided meditation, music and storytelling.

This closing gives you space to recognise the shift you have experienced and leave feeling lighter, grounded and more positive.

Is It a Dance Class?

No.

Shemotion does not teach routines, steps or dance technique.

There is no choreography and no expectation to perform movement correctly.

Dance and ecstatic movement influence parts of the experience, but movement is used as a tool for embodiment, release, expression and emotional state change.

No dance experience is required.

Space to move your way

Who Is Shemotion For?

Shemotion is for women who want a supportive space to move, release and reconnect with themselves.

  • Women who spend too much time in their heads
  • Women carrying stress, tension or emotional heaviness
  • Women who want to reconnect with their bodies
  • Women who miss feeling playful, expressive or alive
  • Women who want movement without choreography or judgement
  • Women who want a supportive women-only environment
  • Women who are curious about embodiment, expressive movement or meditation

No previous dance, movement or meditation experience is required.

Your first session

What to Expect at Your First Shemotion Session

Sessions are women-only, and Katty guides the group from beginning to end. You can come alone or with a friend and simply wear clothing that is comfortable enough to move in.

You are always welcome to modify a movement, make it smaller, pause or stop. There is no pressure to share personal experiences with the group.

Session details and what to bring may vary by event, so check the information provided for the session you choose.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need dance experience?

No. Shemotion is not a dance class and no choreography is taught.

Do I need meditation experience?

No. Katty guides the session from beginning to end.

What if I feel self-conscious moving?

There is no expectation to move in a particular way. You can keep your movements small, pause or rest whenever you need to.

Can I come alone?

Yes. Many women attend Shemotion events on their own.

Is Shemotion women-only?

Yes. Shemotion sessions and events are designed as women-only experiences.
