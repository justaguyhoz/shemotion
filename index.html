Three connected stages

The Shemotion Experience

Each session moves through three connected stages, from guided movement to free expression and grounding stillness.

01

Guided Feminine Movement

Begin with simple, flowing movements inspired by feminine dance and embodiment practices. Warm the body, release physical tension and begin moving out of your head.

02

Free Movement and Expression

Explore intuitive movement with gentle coaching prompts. Move softly, freely, playfully or powerfully without choreography, judgement or pressure to perform.

03

Grounding Meditation

Finish with calming movement and guided meditation to settle the body, integrate the experience and leave feeling lighter and grounded.

Shemotion May Be for You If You:

  • Feel stressed, overwhelmed or emotionally heavy
  • Spend too much time in your head
  • Feel disconnected from your body
  • Want to move and express yourself more freely
  • Want to feel calmer, more confident and grounded

You do not need dance experience or a particular fitness level. Just come as you are.

Meet Katty

I am a certified feminine embodiment coach and former professional dancer with experience in ballroom dance, belly dance, sensual movement and pole dance.

Movement has always been more than exercise for me. It has been a way to express myself, release tension and feel more connected, confident and alive.

Through my embodiment coaching training, I learned how movement can help women listen to their bodies, explore their emotions and reconnect with themselves without judgement or pressure to perform.

Shemotion brings these two parts of my experience together: dance and feminine embodiment.

I created it for women who feel overwhelmed, disconnected or stuck in their heads and want a supportive space to move, express and come back to themselves.

This is not about learning perfect steps. It is about feeling free enough to move as yourself.
