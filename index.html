Three connected stages

The Shemotion Experience

Move. Release. Reconnect with Joy.

01

Get Out of Your Head

We begin by slowing down the mental noise and bringing your attention back into your body.

Through gentle movement, stretching, breath and positive coaching prompts, you start releasing the tension, thoughts and pressure you walked in carrying.

There is nothing to perform and no movement experience required. Just listen to your body and begin to shift your state.

02

Move Into Joy

Then we turn the energy up.

The music builds and the movement becomes freer, wilder and more expressive. This is your space to move however feels good, release tension and reconnect with the playful, powerful and joyful parts of yourself.

Think feminine embodiment meets ecstatic movement - with great music, no choreography and no judgement.

Katty guides the journey with coaching prompts that give the movement intention and help you move beyond simply dancing into genuine emotional release and positive connection.

03

Land in the Afterglow

We finish by bringing the energy gently back down.

You relax into a grounding guided meditation with music and storytelling that reflects the journey you have just experienced - from the noise in your head, through movement and release, into a calmer, lighter and more positive place.

A moment to notice the shift, breathe and leave carrying that feeling with you.

Meet Katty

I am a certified feminine embodiment coach with a focus on helping women reconnect with their bodies through guided movement, breath, coaching prompts and embodiment practices.

Shemotion brings these techniques into a supportive group experience designed to help women move out of their heads, release tension and reconnect with themselves without judgement or pressure to perform.

Sessions move from grounding and body awareness into freer, more expressive movement, using music and positive coaching guidance to help women shift their energy, release what they are carrying and reconnect with joy.

The experience then gently brings the energy back down through breath, grounding and guided reflection, creating space for greater presence, self-connection and emotional release.

Shemotion is not about learning steps. It is about creating space to feel, move, release and reconnect with yourself.
