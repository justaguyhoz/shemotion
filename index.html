A softer kind of release

More Than A Movement Class

Shemotion is a 60-minute feminine embodiment experience designed to support physical release, emotional release, mental release and energetic reconnection.

It is not about performance or perfect steps. It is about letting go, softening, expressing and returning to the body with safety, freedom and presence.

Three connected stages

The Shemotion Experience

Each session moves through three connected stages. The experience begins with guided feminine movement, opens into deeper emotional release, and closes with grounding meditation.

01

Guided Feminine Movement + Physical Release

We begin with guided sensual movement designed to awaken the body, open the hips and activate the pelvic area.

These movements may be inspired by belly dance, feminine flow and embodiment practices, but the focus is not choreography. The focus is connection, release and body awareness.

As the body warms, the movement becomes a physical release - supporting circulation, confidence, energy and freedom while helping women move out of their heads and into their bodies.

02

Free Emotional Expression

The second part of the session is inspired by ecstatic dance, but it is gently guided with feminine embodiment coaching prompts.

Women are invited to move more freely, explore what their body wants to express, and release emotion through natural movement.

This is where the body can let go of stress, frustration, heaviness, frozen feelings and the pressure to "look a certain way."

The movement can be soft, wild, playful, chaotic, sensual or still. There is no right way to move. The invitation is to listen, express and release.

03

Grounding Meditation

Each session closes with a guided meditation to calm the nervous system, soften the body and bring the experience into stillness.

This final stage helps women feel grounded, lighter, uplifted and connected to themselves before stepping back into the world.

About The Coach

Shemotion is guided by Katty, a professional ex-dancer and certified feminine embodiment coach.

From a young age, movement was my way of expressing, releasing and feeling alive. I trained in ballroom dancing, later exploring belly dancing, sensual movement and pole dancing. Over time, I realised that movement was never just physical for me - it was emotional, energetic and deeply healing.

Through my training in feminine embodiment work, I learned how powerful it can be when a woman feels safe enough to listen to her body, trust her inner wisdom and express what she has been holding inside.

Shemotion was born from the fusion of these two worlds - dance and embodiment.

I created this experience for women who feel disconnected, overwhelmed, stuck in their heads or ready to feel more confident, sensual and alive in their bodies.

This is more than a class. It is a journey back to yourself.
