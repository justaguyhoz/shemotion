Meet Katty

I am a certified feminine embodiment coach with a focus on helping women reconnect with their bodies through guided movement, breath and embodiment practices.

Shemotion brings these techniques into a supportive group experience designed to help women release tension, move out of their heads and reconnect with themselves without judgement or pressure to perform.

The sessions combine feminine movement meditation, intuitive movement, breath and grounding practices to create space for greater presence, self-connection and emotional release.

Shemotion is not about learning steps. It is about creating space to feel, move and reconnect with yourself.
